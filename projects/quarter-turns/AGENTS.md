# Quarter turns

## Visual conventions

- Three inks and a fourth for the shapes, in the clip's vars: ground `bg` #f2ede2, main ink `fg` #143d31, hot colour `ac` #ff6a3d, `mid` #f0b7c6. The hot colour marks the moment that matters and little else.
- The name is the clip's var `word`: one to three words, as large as it fits, whole in the held moment.
- Edges cut, nothing fades or glows. What moves eases in and out; nothing waits for the last thing to end.
