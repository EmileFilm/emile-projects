# Quarter turns

A wall of quarter circles turns in step and opens for a name: for design, architecture, food or any calm brand.

**What happens.** Quarter circles *turn* a quarter, one diagonal after the next.

**Beats** (8 s, a loop without a seam)

- A quarter turn every 2 s, rippling from the top left; four make the loop.
- 2.2–3 s the middle opens for the name; it holds until 6.9 s and shuts by 7.7 s.

**What may change**

- The clip's vars in `emile.json`: `word` (one to three words), `bg`, `fg`, `ac`, `mid` (colours: ground, main ink, hot, shapes).
- The length: the beats are shares of the clip's own length.

**What makes it this piece.** The one movement, the order of the beats and the held name. Keep those when making one like it; the picture, the words and the colours are the maker's own.
