# Built in front of you

## Visual conventions

- Three inks and a fourth for the shapes, in the clip's vars: ground `bg` #cfe3ff, main ink `fg` #10224d, hot colour `ac` #2f6bff, `mid` #a3c4ff. The hot colour marks the moment that matters and little else.
- The name is the clip's var `word`: one to three words, as large as it fits, whole in the held moment.
- Edges cut, nothing fades or glows. What moves eases in and out; nothing waits for the last thing to end.
