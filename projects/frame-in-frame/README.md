# Frame in frame

Frames fly out of the picture without end while it floats still in the middle: for sport, music and anything that loops.

**What happens.** Frames *fly* out of the picture.

**Beats** (8 s, a loop without a seam)

- Six frames fly past in the loop and lean one way, then the other.
- 1.3–2.1 s the name pops on its pill; it holds until 7.2 s and goes by 7.8 s.

**What may change**

- The clip's vars in `emile.json`: `word` (one to three words), `bg`, `fg`, `ac` (colours: ground, main ink, hot).
- The picture: `picture()` in `clips/frame-in-frame/clip.html` draws it on a 720 × 1080 sheet. For a real one, add `<img id="photo" hidden src="../../assets/<file>">` before the canvas.
- The length: the beats are shares of the clip's own length.

**What makes it this piece.** The one movement, the order of the beats and the held name. Keep those when making one like it; the picture, the words and the colours are the maker's own.
