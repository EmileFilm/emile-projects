# Say the film

A ball, a slam and a run of short chapters cut on the beat, landing on the name: for a product's launch reel.

**What happens.** A reel of *chapters*, each one idea on its own ground, cut hard on the beat.

**Beats** (15.2 s at 150 BPM, a beat every 0.4 s)

- 0–1.6 s an orange ball bounces across a dark ground and comes to rest.
- 1.6–3.2 s on orange, the title slams in a word a beat.
- 3.2–10.4 s six numbered chapters, 1.2 s each (the last 0.8 s): a chat line that turns into shapes, clips dropping onto tracks, a playhead sweeping a ring of dots, a frame circled and cut, a look picked, cards counting to 100%.
- 10.4–12.0 s seven icons, one every half beat.
- 12.0–15.2 s the ball lands as the accent on the name, the line comes in word by word, the button pops; it holds.
- Music at the same tempo, and one hit as the line lands.

**What may change**

- The words: the title in `clips/title-slam/clip.html`, each chapter's number and line in its clip, the name and the line in `clips/icon-burst/clip.html`.
- The colours and type: the Visual conventions in `AGENTS.md` name them all; the clips use those values.
- The logo: `assets/brand/Émile/logo-dark.png`; put yours in its place.
- The music: `assets/music/`, or have the AI score it again at 150 BPM.

**What makes it this piece.** One idea a chapter, each on its own ground; every entrance, morph and hit on a beat; the ball that opens it is the accent that closes it. Keep those when making one like it; the words, the chapters' subjects and the colours are the maker's own.
