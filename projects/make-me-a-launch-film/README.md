# Make me a launch film

A typed line that turns into clips on a timeline, cut on the beat at 160 BPM: for an AI product's launch reel.

**What happens.** One idea a scene, each handing on to the next on a beat: what is said becomes the film.

**Beats** (15 s at 160 BPM, a beat every 0.375 s)

- 0–1.5 s an ember ball bounces along a cream line, leaving a dotted trail.
- 1.5–3 s on orange, "SAY IT." slams in, the ball its full stop.
- 3–6 s "Make me a launch film" is typed; its words lift off and drop onto three tracks as clips, a word a half beat.
- 6–7.5 s the tracks stack in 3D: picture, words, look.
- 7.5–9 s one frame takes look after look: clean, halftone, risograph, glow.
- 9–10.5 s the sun is circled and its clip cut while the playhead runs to 00:15.
- 10.5–11.6 s flashes, one every half beat.
- 11.6–15 s a pill whips across into the end card: the logo, the name, the line word by word, one low hit as it lands; it holds.

**What may change**

- The words: the typed line in `clips/prompt/clip.html`, the title in `clips/say-it/clip.html`, the name and the line in `clips/pill-whip/clip.html`, the small labels in every clip.
- The colours and type: the Visual conventions in `AGENTS.md` name them all; the clips use those values.
- The logo: `assets/brand/Émile/logo-dark.png`; put yours in its place.
- The music: none goes with a template; have the AI score it at 160 BPM (the prompt says what it was).

**What makes it this piece.** The line typed at the start is what the film goes on to make; every landing on a beat, everything else moving smoothly between; the ball that opens it is the full stop that closes it. Keep those when making one like it; the words, the product and the colours are the maker's own.
