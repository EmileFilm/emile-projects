# Round and round

A name circles a picture in rings that turn against each other, then takes its place: for a logo, a badge or a soft launch.

**What happens.** Rings of the name *turn* round the picture.

**Beats** (8 s, a loop without a seam)

- Every ring turns a whole number of repeats, so it loops.
- 3.2–4 s a disc opens over the picture with the name whole; it holds until 6.9 s and closes by 7.7 s.

**What may change**

- The clip's vars in `emile.json`: `word` (one to three words), `bg`, `fg`, `ac` (colours: ground, main ink, hot).
- The picture: `picture()` in `clips/round-and-round/clip.html` draws it on a 720 × 1080 sheet. For a real one, add `<img id="photo" hidden src="../../assets/<file>">` before the canvas.
- The length: the beats are shares of the clip's own length.

**What makes it this piece.** The one movement, the order of the beats and the held name. Keep those when making one like it; the picture, the words and the colours are the maker's own.
