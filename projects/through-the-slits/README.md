# Through the slits

Nine slits open one after another on a picture, as in Reynaud’s drum, and a name stands in the middle: for a trailer, a place or a night.

**What happens.** Slits *open* on the picture, left to right.

**Beats** (8 s, a loop without a seam)

- 0.2–2.8 s they open; a third of each is always open, so no frame is black.
- 2.4–3.2 s the three in the middle close into a column for the name; it holds until 5.8 s.
- 6–8 s they close back to thirds.

**What may change**

- The clip's vars in `emile.json`: `word` (one to three words), `bg`, `fg`, `ac` (colours: ground, main ink, hot).
- The picture: `picture()` in `clips/through-the-slits/clip.html` draws it on a 720 × 1080 sheet. For a real one, add `<img id="photo" hidden src="../../assets/<file>">` before the canvas.
- The length: the beats are shares of the clip's own length.

**What makes it this piece.** The one movement, the order of the beats and the held name. Keep those when making one like it; the picture, the words and the colours are the maker's own.
