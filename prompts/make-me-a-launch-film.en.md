Colours: film black #100f0e for dark grounds, with a soft brown glow (#2d2823) behind the centre; dark panel #1b1917 for cards and tracks; cream #f7f4ee for words on dark and for light grounds; ember orange #d9541e, the one accent. Nothing else.
